Xu is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Florida International University [(FIU)](https://www.fiu.edu), co-advised by [Dr. Dongsheng Luo](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/faculty/profile/8626/luo-dongsheng) and [Dr. Mo Sha](https://users.cs.fiu.edu/~msha/). His research areas are **AI4Science**, **XAI**, **LLM Agents**, **Time Series Learning**, and **Graph Modeling**.
He received his B.E. in Electronic Science and Technology from Chongqing University [(CQU)](https://www.cqu.edu.cn) in 2018, and his M.E. in Control Science and Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China [(UESTC)](https://www.uestc.edu.cn) in 2021, where his supervisor was [Dr. Yali Zheng](http://www.uestc-vml.net/) and his research topic was human mesh recovery.

**He expects to complete his Ph.D. in Summer 2027 and is on the 2026–2027 job market for faculty, research scientist, and applied scientist positions. His [CV](/files/Xu_Zheng_CV.pdf) is available here.**
